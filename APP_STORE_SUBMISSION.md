## Description (4000자 제한)

```
A 1-minute morning ritual for a better day.

Novakitz is where your mornings go. Tap the circle, choose the pebble that matches how you woke up, and you are done. On a rushed morning that is the whole ritual, and it still counts.

WHEN YOU REMEMBER A DREAM

Press and hold the circle to go straight to writing, skipping the pebble. Novakitz reads the dream back to you — the images in it, the feeling underneath, what it might be circling around — in plain language rather than jargon.

Your dreams become easier to remember when you give them a place to return to. Most people do not forget their dreams because their memory is poor. They forget because nothing was waiting to receive them.

WHAT BUILDS UP

• A calendar where every morning is a coloured pebble, so a month has a shape you can see at a glance
• Reflection — the moods that keep coming back, the symbols that repeat across your dreams, and how the last few weeks have leaned
• Monthly Review — a written reading of your month, the archetypes that surfaced in it, and something to carry into the next one
• A daily affirmation drawn from what you actually recorded, not from a generic list

The longer you keep it, the more it has to say. Understanding yourself is not one insight that arrives; it is what accumulates when you keep noticing.

A STREAK THAT FORGIVES

One missed day each month does not break your streak. This is a morning ritual, not a test.

FREE AND PRO

Free includes the morning mood check-in, the calendar, the streak, and 7 dream interpretations each month.

Pro adds daily dream interpretations, the full Monthly Review with its AI reading and discovered archetypes, and your complete history.

Pro is $5.99 per month or $49.99 per year.

PRIVACY

Your dreams and moods are yours. They are stored in your own account and are never sold, shared, or used to advertise to you.
```

### Notes

```
HOW TO USE THE APP

The home screen is a single circle.

1. TAP the circle once. Eight pebbles appear — this is the morning mood check-in. Choose the one that matches how you woke up. This is the core daily action and takes about five seconds.

2. PRESS AND HOLD the circle for about one second to skip the pebble and go straight to writing a dream. This shortcut exists for people who wake up with a dream they want to write down before it fades.

3. The menu button in the top right opens Inner Journal, Reflection, Monthly Review and Pricing.

The demo account above already has about a month of recorded mornings, so the calendar, Reflection and Monthly Review all have real data to show. Open the menu and choose Calendar, then step back to August to see a full month.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

Two auto-renewing subscriptions unlock Pro: monthly and yearly. The paywall is reachable from the menu under Pricing, and from the Monthly Review screen. Restore Purchases is on the Pricing screen.

The free tier includes the mood check-in, the calendar and 7 dream interpretations per month. The demo account is provisioned as Pro so that the Monthly Review can be reviewed without a purchase.

DREAM INTERPRETATION

Interpretations are generated by an AI model and are written as reflective prompts, not as advice. The app makes no medical, psychological or diagnostic claims and does not present itself as a health or medical service.
```
